# Into the Mist

Penultimate base-game dungeon. Source: the official wiki,
[Into the Mist](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Into_the_Mist) and its
[Guide](https://wiki.melvoridle.com/w/Into_the_Mist/Guide) (community guide written for v1.3.1).

## What it really is

- **20 Afflicted monsters** picked at random (normal stats and specials, +20% damage reduction, can apply
  Affliction). Slayer area effects do not apply to them.
- Then a **3-phase final boss**, the Mysterious Figure. Each phase uses one style and only takes damage from
  that same style. Combat **pauses before each phase** so you can swap sets; a button starts the phase.
- **Dungeon Equipment Swapping** (shop upgrade) is mandatory to swap sets inside the dungeon.

## Affliction

Every enemy attack (hit or miss) has a 70% chance to add a stack, up to 50. Each stack removes 1% max HP and so
lowers the auto-eat threshold. Stacks reset when the monster dies or you leave combat. This is the first dungeon
that usually needs manual eating: you survive as long as the boss's max hit stays under your max HP.

## Phases

| Phase | Style | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Afflicted waves 1-20 | any | Best magic set or swap per combat triangle; not dangerous |
| Phase 1 | Melee | Hardest. Protect from Melee (caps hit chance at 20%, limits *Reap and Return* healing). Fast 1h weapon to drop *Mark of Death* stacks quickly. Idle needs a weapon under 2.6 s and enough HP/DR to take a 600 hit at 50% HP. |
| Phase 2 | Ranged | Easiest. Battleheart + Rigour. Idle needs to take a 772 hit at 50% HP. |
| Phase 3 | Magic | See the guide. |

Never manually eat while *Mark of Death* is on you when idling: finish an attack first to drop it.

## Rewards

Lore books, the **passive equipment slot**, Dark Waters added to Master Slayer tasks; 5 clears give the pet
Pablo, the Agility item cost reduction upgrade and (with Slayer 95) Stronghold of the Gods.

## Readiness checklist

- [ ] Dungeon Equipment Swapping bought
- [ ] One set per style, each simulated against its phase at or under the death threshold
- [ ] Enough HP and damage reduction for each phase's max hit at 50% HP (to idle) or a manual eating plan
- [ ] Whale-class food, Diamond Luck Potion, Dragon + Minotaur/Centaur/Witch
